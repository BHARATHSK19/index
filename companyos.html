02 The reality of Indian enterprises

Why generic SaaS tools fail growing Indian businesses.

Legacy & Fragmented Work

Siloed, Unaudited & Slow

  • WhatsApp Approvals Critical financial purchases, discount waivers, and contract terms approved casually over chat apps with no permanent paper trail or audit logs.
  • Standalone Attendance Machines Biometric finger logs exported manually on USB pen drives at month-end, causing payroll calculation delays and disputes.
  • Spreadsheet Delegation Matrix When a department head is on leave, vendor invoices and operational approvals stall indefinitely due to lack of automatic delegation.
  • Executive Blindspots Directors wait until the 15th of next month to understand cash positions, pending liabilities, and true divisional profitability.

OJIX OS Connected Model

Real-Time, Transparent & Scalable

  • Cryptographic Approval Trails Every financial approval, budget deviation, and operational sign-off is logged with timestamp, user ID, and automated compliance policies.
  • Live IoT Attendance Telemetry Facial recognition and biometric turnstiles push telemetry continuously to automated payroll rosters, enforcing shift and overtime rules instantly.
  • Smart Escalation & Matrix Delegations Multi-tier approval thresholds automatically route to secondary managers or deputy directors after designated SLA timeouts.
  • Executive Live Cockpit Immediate, tamper-proof insight into live receivables, vendor commitments, staff availability, and department operating margins.

How does OJIX OS integrate with existing biometric attendance machines and turnstiles?

OJIX OS connects seamlessly with industry-standard biometric turnstiles, RFID sensors, and facial recognition devices via edge gateways. Clock-in data synchronizes within sub-second intervals into central attendance rosters with automated shift rules and zero manual intervention.

Does OJIX OS handle Indian statutory payroll compliance like PF, ESI, and TDS?

Yes. OJIX OS embeds all statutory calculation slabs for Employee Provident Fund (EPF), ESIC, Professional Tax across Indian states, and dual-regime Income Tax TDS. Monthly payroll generates one-click bank transfer NEFT/RTGS files and automated Form 16 statements.

Can OJIX OS model complex multi-level approval hierarchies for CapEx and expenses?

Yes. The platform provides a visual delegation of authority matrix where purchase orders, travel expenses, leave requests, and vendor agreements route automatically based on financial thresholds, departmental reporting lines, and escalation timers.

Is our corporate data hosted in India with sovereign compliance?

Yes. All OJIX OS instances are deployed in Tier-IV MeitY-empanelled data centers in Bengaluru and Mumbai. Your enterprise operations data remains 100% sovereign, adhering strictly to the DPDP Act 2023 with AES-256 encryption at rest.

Can we migrate our existing spreadsheets, legacy ERP, and Tally data into OJIX OS?

Yes. OJIX provides white-glove engineering migration. We sanitize, structure, and import existing employee masters, historical financial ledgers, inventory SKU lists, and vendor contracts with zero operational downtime.
