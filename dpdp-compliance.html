02 Architectural imperatives

The Six Pillars of DPDP 2025 Enterprise Compliance.

Indian law enforces strict technical boundaries. Here is how enterprise database and application layers must be designed to withstand regulatory audits by the Data Protection Board of India (DPBI).

01

Itemized Consent Architecture

Section 6(1) bans bundled, pre-ticked consent. Systems must record verifiable timestamps, specific lawful purposes, and offer notices in English and 22 Indian constitutional languages with instant withdrawal APIs.

  • Rule 3 Notice Engine
  • Opt-Out Webhooks
  • Immutable Consent Logs

02

Automated Retention & Erasure

Section 8(7) requires automatic data erasure as soon as the specified commercial purpose is fulfilled. Databases must enforce automated lifecycle policies that delete or anonymize records without manual intervention.

  • TTL Row Policies
  • Cryptographic Shredding
  • Anonymization Pipelines

03

Sovereign Security Safeguards

Section 8(5) mandates reasonable security measures to prevent personal data breaches. Implementation requires AES-256 encryption at rest, tenant key rotation, TLS 1.3 in transit, and role-based access control (RBAC).

  • Tenant Key Isolation
  • Zero-Trust Network
  • HMAC Tamper Proofing

04

48-Hour Grievance Redressal

Data Principals have a statutory right to grievance redressal. The enterprise must publish a designated Grievance Officer and implement ticketing workflows guaranteeing acknowledgement within 48 hours.

  • Section 13 Compliance
  • 48h SLA Tracking
  • DPBI Escalation Ready

05

72-Hour Breach Notification

Section 8(6) requires immediate notification of any breach to the Data Protection Board of India and affected individuals. Automated SIEM anomaly detection must alert security teams in real time.

  • Section 8(6) Alerts
  • Forensic Audit Trails
  • DPBI Notice Templates

06

Protection of Minor & Child Data

Section 9 strictly prohibits tracking, behavioral monitoring, or targeted advertising directed at children under 18. Verifiable parental consent mechanisms must be enforced at registration.

  • Verifiable Parental Consent
  • Zero Behavioral Profiling
  • Campus Data Hardening

05 Regulatory & technical FAQs

Direct answers for DPOs, CTOs & General Counsels.

Statutory interpretations and engineering best practices under India’s digital data protection framework.

What are the core technical obligations for Data Fiduciaries under the DPDP Rules 2025?

The DPDP Rules 2025 mandate: (1) itemized, non-bundled consent notices available in English and the 22 Eighth Schedule languages, (2) technical mechanisms for automated consent withdrawal, (3) data erasure and anonymization upon purpose fulfillment, (4) reasonable security safeguards including AES-256 encryption and tenant isolation, and (5) designated Grievance Officers with guaranteed 48-hour response SLAs.

What is the statutory timeline for reporting a personal data breach under the DPDP Act?

Under Section 8(6) of the DPDP Act and the DPDP Rules 2025, Data Fiduciaries must notify the Data Protection Board of India (DPBI) and affected Data Principals within 72 hours of becoming aware of a confirmed personal data breach.

Does the DPDP Act require all personal data to be stored strictly in India?

While cross-border transfer to non-blacklisted jurisdictions is permitted under Section 16, Indian sovereign cloud residency (such as MeitY-empanelled Tier-IV data centers in Bengaluru and Mumbai) is critical for Significant Data Fiduciaries (SDFs), government agencies, critical infrastructure, and defense/legal data.
