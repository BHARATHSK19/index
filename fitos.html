05 Fitness operations FAQs

Clarifications on FitOS deployment.

Direct answers regarding multi-modal check-ins, multi-branch scaling, lead CRM execution, personal trainer commissions, and Indian tax compliance.

info@ojix.in →
What check-in methods are supported across front-desk and turnstiles?

FitOS supports multi-modal check-in options including dynamic QR code scanning on the member mobile app, RFID/NFC smart cards and key fobs, integrated edge biometric turnstiles, and front-desk receptionist member search with fast manual check-in overrides.

How does FitOS handle multi-branch chains and franchise locations?

FitOS provides a centralized multi-branch hierarchy allowing enterprise administrators to define global membership plans, enforce franchise-level brand standards, configure cross-studio access permissions for members, and review consolidated multi-branch financial reports.

How does the built-in lead CRM manage walk-ins and trial booking workflows?

FitOS captures enquiries automatically from web forms, social ad campaigns, and front-desk walk-in registrations. Sales staff can assign trial slots, track customizable pipeline stages, and trigger automated WhatsApp follow-up reminders to accelerate membership conversion.

How are personal trainer schedules and tiered commissions calculated?

Trainers access a dedicated mobile portal to schedule PT sessions, view client assessment history, and log completed appointments. FitOS automatically validates session completion and computes tiered commission slabs, eliminating month-end payroll discrepancies.

How does FitOS ensure Indian tax compliance and recurring fee collection?

FitOS natively generates GST-compliant invoices under SAC 9997 with customizable tax breakdowns for memberships, personal training, and cafe retail. It supports recurring UPI Autopay mandates and domestic gateway integrations to automate renewals and reduce revenue leakage.
