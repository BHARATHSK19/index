— Questions

Clear answers for . decision makers

Detailed answers on enterprise architecture, statutory DPDP compliance, deterministic AI pipelines, and commercial engagement terms.

What distinguishes an OJIX Operating System from generic SaaS tools?

Generic SaaS tools force businesses to adapt real-world operations to rigid, one-size-fits-all software that produces disconnected silos. OJIX designs and engineers sovereign operating systems tailored around your organization’s actual roles, approval hierarchies, legal regulations, and operational reality—bringing cross-functional visibility, deterministic workflows, and executive clarity into one unified, sovereign environment.

Can OJIX integrate with or migrate legacy ERPs like SAP, Oracle, or Tally?

Yes. OJIX platforms feature enterprise bi-directional connectors, transactional event streams, and certified API adapters for legacy ERPs (SAP ECC/S4 HANA, Oracle EBS, Microsoft Dynamics), accounting suites (Tally Prime, Zoho Books), Core Banking Systems, and statutory government gateways (GSTN, MCA21, e-Way bill). We can operate as an intelligent orchestration layer on top of existing software or execute staged, zero-downtime database migrations with automated mathematical reconciliation.

How modular is the deployment? Can an enterprise start with one department?

Every OJIX operating system is engineered around a composable micro-kernel architecture. Organizations frequently start with a single mission-critical capability—such as ProcureOS for automated 3-way invoice matching or Nyaya Sethu for contract automation—and progressively activate financial ledgering, inventory, or workforce management modules without code rewrites or platform migrations.

How does OJIX comply with India’s DPDP Act, 2023 and the DPDP Rules, 2025?

OJIX operates under strict compliance with the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 and MeitY DPDP Rules, 2025. All personal data is processed under explicit consent with itemized purpose notices, unbundled cookie preferences, compatibility with registered Consent Managers, AES-256/TLS 1.3 encryption in sovereign Indian data centers, and a designated Grievance Redressal Officer reachable at info@ojix.in with a guaranteed 48-hour response SLA and 72-hour breach reporting protocols.

Where is enterprise data stored and processed in OJIX platforms?

100% of enterprise client data resides in sovereign MeitY-empanelled Tier-IV data centers physically located within India. We enforce client-isolated VPC tenancies, encryption at rest via AES-256-GCM, and TLS 1.3 in transit. OJIX commercial contracts provide strict statutory guarantees of zero cross-border telemetry leakage and zero third-party data monetization.

What role-based access controls (RBAC) and audit capabilities are provided?

Every user interaction, state change, approval step, data query, and API modification is cryptographically hashed and recorded to an immutable append-only audit trail. Granular RBAC and Attribute-Based Access Control (ABAC) allow security administrators to restrict data visibility by department, geographic jurisdiction, role seniority, or client mandate.

How does OJIX ensure reliable AI workflow automation without hallucinations?

OJIX builds deterministic, human-in-the-loop AI pipelines. LLMs and foundation models are strictly confined to scoped sensory tasks: OCR document extraction, natural language classification, drafting, and semantic clause comparison. All mathematical computations, financial ledgers, policy verifications, and ERP database updates are executed through auditable deterministic code with mandatory human approval gates.

5. Statutory Rights of the Data Principal (Sections 11–14 & Rule 5)

Under Chapter III of the DPDP Act 2023 and Rule 5 of the DPDP Rules 2025, Data Principals enjoy comprehensive, enforceable digital rights:

01 Right to Access

Obtain a summary of personal data being processed, a description of processing activities, and identities of any data processors or third parties with whom data was shared.

02 Right to Correction & Completion

Request correction of inaccurate information, completion of incomplete records, or updating of your business contact information.

03 Right to Erasure

Mandate permanent deletion of personal data once the inquiry purpose is fulfilled or consent is withdrawn, unless statutory retention is required by Indian law.

04 Right of Grievance Redressal

Access our designated Grievance Officer with guaranteed acknowledgment within 48 hours and final substantive resolution within thirty (30) days.

05 Right to Nominate (Rule 6)

Nominate another individual through an electronic nomination facility who shall, in the event of death or incapacity, exercise your data rights.

06 No Discriminatory Treatment

OJIX will never deny services, alter quality of service, or charge discriminatory fees to any Data Principal exercising statutory DPDP rights.

Transparency · DPDP Act 2023 & DPDP Rules 2025

Cookie & Local Storage Policy

This Cookie Policy outlines the operational deployment of cookies and local storage on ojix.in by OJIX Engineering & Technology LLP in compliance with India's DPDP Act, 2023 and DPDP Rules, 2025.

What are cookies & browser storage?

Cookies and client-side storage technologies are small cryptographic or text strings placed on your device to maintain secure session integrity, remember your consent states, and facilitate efficient digital platform operation.

Classification of cookies deployed

  • Strictly Necessary (Legitimate Operational Need): Session security tokens, anti-CSRF protections, and your explicit cookie consent state stored in localStorage (ojix_dpdp_cookie_consent). These cookies cannot be turned off as they are critical to fundamental web service delivery.
  • Performance & Diagnostics (Affirmative Consent Required): Anonymized metrics measuring page load times, device viewport categories, and asset delivery performance. These are never shared with third-party advertising brokers or cross-site tracking graphs.

Granular Management & Consent Manager Support

In adherence to Rule 4 of the DPDP Rules 2025, you can review, toggle, or withdraw your cookie consent at any moment using our or through any registered Consent Manager registered with the DPBI.
