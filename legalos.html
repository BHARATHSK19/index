05 Legal practice FAQs

Everything you need to know about Nyaya Sethu.

Clear, direct answers regarding judicial integrations, data migration, security guarantees, and chamber deployment.

info@ojix.in →
Does Nyaya Sethu automatically sync with e-Courts Services and High Court cause lists?

Yes. Nyaya Sethu connects with e-Courts Services, District Courts, State High Courts, NCLT, and the Supreme Court of India. It automatically updates daily cause lists, court hall numbers, item sequence numbers, and interim orders directly into your firm's case dashboard.

How does Nyaya Sethu ensure client confidentiality and Bar Council compliance?

Nyaya Sethu is hosted exclusively in sovereign Tier-IV data centers inside India, adhering strictly to the Bar Council of India professional standards and the Digital Personal Data Protection (DPDP) Act, 2023. Client briefs are encrypted using AES-256 with tenant-isolated cryptographic keys, preventing cross-tenant access or external data harvesting.

How does the statutory limitation tracking engine prevent missed court deadlines?

Nyaya Sethu embeds the statutory schedules of the Limitation Act, 1963, Commercial Courts Act, 2015, and arbitration rules. The system calculates precise filing windows, counts court vacations and gazetted holidays, and triggers multi-tier escalation notifications across senior partners and briefing associates.

Can our firm migrate existing litigation files from spreadsheets and consumer cloud drives?

Yes. OJIX provides end-to-end data migration engineering. We sanitize, structure, and ingest historical case dossiers, past orders, client contact books, and fee ledgers from Excel, Google Sheets, or legacy servers into Nyaya Sethu with zero operational disruption.

Is Nyaya Sethu available as a single-tenant private cloud or on-premise installation?

Yes. While we offer managed sovereign cloud hosting in Bengaluru and Mumbai, enterprise firms and senior advocate chambers can deploy Nyaya Sethu within a dedicated private VPC or on-premise infrastructure under full institutional ownership.
