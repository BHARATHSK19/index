How long does an enterprise implementation typically take?

Most custom builds move from architecture sprint to live MVP staging in 4 to 8 weeks, followed by iterative multi-department rollout.

Do we own the full source code and intellectual property?

Yes. 100% of all code, configuration scripts, and documentation are transferred to your organization with zero vendor lock-in.

Where is our data stored and processed?

All production servers and backups are hosted in Tier-IV sovereign data centers located in Bengaluru and Mumbai, fully compliant with the DPDP Act 2023.

How does your team handle system maintenance and SLAs?

We provide guaranteed SLA contracts including 24/7 production monitoring, vulnerability patching, and guaranteed response times.

Can this integrate with our existing Tally, ERP, or biometric turnstiles?

Yes. We build native connectors for Tally Prime, SAP, Zoho, biometric attendance gateways, and Indian banking portals.
